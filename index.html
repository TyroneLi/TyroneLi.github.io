Biography

I am a visiting PhD student at the Computer Vision and Geometry Group (CVG), ETH Zurich, Switzerland, under the supervision of Prof. Marc Pollefeys, and a PhD student at the Multimedia and Human Understanding Group (MHUG), University of Trento, Italy, under the supervision of Prof. Nicu Sebe. Previously, I was a research scientist in computer vision at Meituan, working closely with Dr. Lin Ma and Dr. Zequn Jie on label-efficient 2D/3D/4D detection and segmentation. Before that, I received a dual B.Sc. degree (major: Applied Physics) and an M.Sc. degree in Computer Science from Shenzhen University.

Research Interest

I work on computer vision and deep learning. My recent research aims to build machines that can see, reason about, and interact with the geometric and physical world through a unified end-to-end paradigm built on representation and generative learning. Topics I am exploring include:

  • Multi-Modal Perception, Reasoning and Planning
  • Spatial Foundation Models
  • Generative Models
  • Label-Efficient Learning in 2D, 3D and 4D
